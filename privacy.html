Privacy

Your information, kept quiet.

A plain account of what we collect and what we do with it.

On this pageWhat we collectHow we use itSharingYour choicesContact

What we collect.

This site does not use advertising trackers. When you request access or email us, we receive what you choose to send, such as your name, email address, phone number and how we know each other. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and reliability.

How we use it.

We use your information to respond to you, to review a request for investor access, to keep records required of us, and to protect the site. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing.

We share information only with service providers who help us operate (for example, email and hosting), with our affiliate Real Quick Funds where needed to respond to you, or when required by law. Investors who are approved receive a separate privacy notice with their offering documents.

Your choices.

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to any records we must keep. Email us and we will respond.
