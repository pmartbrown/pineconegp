What we do

One niche, said plainly. No forecasts, no figures we can’t document, no adjectives doing heavy lifting.

We fund the gap between signing and closing: boring credit, underwritten like an insurer would underwrite it.

An insurer starts by assuming something will go wrong, then decides what it is willing to cover. We work the same way. Each transaction is short, defined before capital moves, and settled through a licensed title company or closing attorney. The excitement, such as it is, belongs to someone else.

Approach · Duration

Short.

Capital is committed for the length of a closing, not a market cycle. We are not tied to the stock market or to where home prices go next year.

Approach · Paper

Secured.

Each deal is documented with purpose-built contracts and, where applicable, recorded filings.²

² Documentation varies by deal type and state.
Approach · Settlement

Seen.

Funds move through a licensed title company or closing attorney, with a settlement statement every party can read.

Approach · Posture

Like an insurer.

Assume the deal slips. Decide what we would cover if it did. We turn down far more than we take.
