Paul Brown

Founder & Managing Member

Paul brings decades of experience in financial services, preceded by a career in mechanical engineering and robotics. He served as international Chief Underwriting Officer for a Fortune 200 company, directing business lines that generated approximately $5 billion in revenue and holding multiple board positions. Earlier, he managed a $26 billion mortgage servicing portfolio. In 2023 he turned full time to real estate investing and private lending, and he founded Real Quick Funds and Pinecone Capital.
